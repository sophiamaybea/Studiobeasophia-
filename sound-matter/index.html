For a composer or band site, this can sit behind the title, become the hero itself, or run as a full-screen listening room.

The music is not decoration.

The engine interprets sound as physical forces. Low frequencies carry mass. Midrange stretches elastic lines. Transients strike the page. High frequencies shed dust and graphite. Spectral brightness changes the material language.

The marks have inertia, spring tension, damping and memory, so the image evolves continuously rather than redrawing a random picture on every frame.

Embedding is deliberately simple. Host an audio file with CORS enabled and point this page at it:

?embed=1&src=https://example.com/song.mp3&title=Track%20Name

Nothing generative is pretending to be the composer. The intelligence is in the translation between the artist's music and the site's visual behaviour.
