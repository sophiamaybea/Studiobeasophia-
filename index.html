This reconstruction treats the poster as a physical paste-up rather than a flat screenshot. Type, figures, doodles and photocopied matter remain separate objects you can lift, disturb and return.

The default state stays close to the supplied reference: off-white stock, crushed black photocopy, oversized vertical type, a standing silhouette and messy hand-cut hierarchy.

No framework. No external runtime. Pointer, touch and keyboard controls share one movement model. Reduced-motion preferences are respected.
